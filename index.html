Diego Sandoval
Neuroscience

My goal is to pursue a career in healthcare by studying neuroscience, gaining hands-on medical experience, and continuing to develop the skills needed to make a positive impact on people's lives.

My name is Diego Sandoval, and I am a student at Patriot High School in Jurupa Valley, California. Throughout high school, I have had many opportunities to learn new things, meet different people, and discover what I want to do in the future. I would describe myself as someone who is motivated, hardworking, and always willing to try something new. I enjoy challenging myself and finding ways to improve, both in school and in my personal life. One of my biggest interests is healthcare and medicine. Over the past few years, I have become more interested in understanding how the human body works and how medical professionals help people. One experience that really helped me explore this interest was participating in a Medical Assistant Bootcamp. During the program, I was introduced to different medical skills, including blood draws and other hands-on procedures. It was a great opportunity because I got to experience what working in a medical enviroment could be like. It also helped me understand that working in healthcare takes patience, responsibility, and the ability to work with others. Outside of the classroom, I have also been involved in different school organizations, including the California Scholarship Federation (CSF), AVID, and Link Crew. Through these activities, I have had the chance to take on leadership roles, work with other students, and help organize school events. These experiences have taught me how important teamwork and communication are. They have also helped me become more confident when speaking with others and taking responsibility for different tasks. I enjoy being part of organizations where I can help make a difference in my school community. As I get closer to starting the next chapter of my education, I hope to continue studying neuroscience in college. I find the brain and how it works very interesting, and I would like to learn more about how it affects the way people think, feel, and behave. I am also interested in exploring different careers in the medical field. One of my goals is to become an EMT after college so I can gain real-world experience working with patients and learn more about emergency medicine. I am still exploring my options, but I know I want a career where I can help people. When I am not focused on school, I enjoy spending time with my family and friends, playing guitar, and trying new things. My family is an important part of my life, and they motivate me to keep working toward my goals. I also believe that there is always room to grow and learn something new. Overall, I am excited about what the future holds. I know I still have a lot to learn, but I am willing to put in the effort and take advantage of the opportunities that come my way. My goal is to build a career that I enjoy while also making a positive impact on the lives of others.
